A Mote of Love

By Vera, August 13, 2026

Your visits are delights, my love When hand-in-hand we wade into our memory That for an afternoon I forget all about my death And how each day my fatal pain grows worse Oh darling, my darling, it’s all so precious now Your laugh, your hands, your warmth and warming blood

Today my cough rang red with tangy blood But through my fear I held fast onto your love I cannot break, I must not crumble now For my ghost must be made of memory I’ll haunt you soon, a haunt I won’t make worse Oh dignity, dear dignity, come with me to death

The wont of life is not to dwell on death But death’s sweet tar is thickening my blood Red and black, each beat sustains and renders worse Staining dark my brain and bones, but cannot touch my love Oh mind, poor mind, hold fast to memory I feel it slipping, I feel it going now

Where is my love, where is my lover now I am alone in here with death I cannot see, I cannot reach the memory But I know my love, I feel them in my blood Oh nurse, kind nurse, won’t you call my love Before I fade, before my mind grows worse

Oh God, dear God, this pain is so much worse But I can feel, I can sense you now I do not know, I do not know your name, my love Just as I do not know death I must believe I’m more than flesh and blood But who am I without my memory

I must let go, I am not memory I am myself, and I am nothing worse Oh life, sweet life, I offer you my blood I cannot flee, I cannot slip this timeless now I can see, I can see the face of death And in its eyes, oh God, a mote of love

My stilling blood, my weary memory I scream my shapeless love, it’s simple but no worse There is naught but now, all is love and death
