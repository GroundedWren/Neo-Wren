Mirror, Mirror [WIP]

The Salty Breeze - Chapter 7/?

Jax is out to everyone but Kaufmo and Caine, and has just entered a romantic relationship with Ribbit. Before she got a chance to tell the others, two new members joined the Circus at the same time: Zooble and Poe (fka Raven in BCS).

When the cast tried to explain the situation to the new arrivals, Caine effectively went rampant and stated his belief that any personality change in the cast was a sign of his failure and a precursor to abstraction.

To fix this for Jax, Caine performed a rollback on her memories, making her forget everything after she joined the circus. Then, to demonstrate how well he knows everyone, he launched them into this adventure.

The scene was set. A dense smattering of stars in white, blue, red, and yellow drifted lazily forward through the near-black void of space.

“Wh-, no, I’m not reading this,” protested Jax, “not until you explain- argh!!” A terrible, burning electricity pulled at the muscles on the back of his neck as the metallic agonizer there affixed hummed uncaringly.

“Come on, Jax, this only works if you play along!” said Caine, his tone betraying a flavor of anxiety not dissimilar to that of a friend trying to sell him on a new television show. “Humor me, please.”

Jax sighed. I must’ve died. I’m dead and in hell. Figures. “Fine.” He looked down at the script and began reading.

“Space - the final frontier.” His words resounded dramatically across the panning star-field as a triumphant, hopeful melody began. Yeesh. “These are the voyages of the TAD Enterprise, I guess.” A spaceship swooshed through the aether and entered the orbit of a red gaseous planet heretofore unseen. “It’s five-year mission to explore strange new worlds, to seek out new life and new civilizations - to boldly go where no m-, no one has gone before.” The music swelled dramatically.

STAR TREK

CREATED
                         BY CAINE

STARRING
JAX as CAPTAIN KIRK

ALSO STARRING
RIBBIT as MR. SPOCK

AND
ZOOBLE as DR. McCOY

Jax was dressed in his captain’s finest; it was the same command gold as his daily uniform, but it was adorned with brush-like epaulettes and ribbons on the its cuffs. This mission was a diplomatic one, a trade discussion with the Halkan Council to secure the Federation a new source of diglucose crystals to assist in outfitting a new fleet of ships set to begin exploration along the Galactic Barrier in the years to come.

The away team was small and light - as a deliberate gesture to the Halkans, they had left behind the mandated two phasers that Starfleet regulation insisted go planet-side with any senior officers; the previous day’s round of negotiations had made clear that this was a society which valued nonviolence and harm-reduction above all else. Still, two red-shirted mannequins from Poe’s security team were present at hir insistence, “just in case”.

The lanky rabbit captain was accompanied by Ribbit, his chief science officer, wearing her standard blue uniform and clutching a tricorder more out of habit than with any immediate purpose. Her red bow was re-tied into a Starfleet insignia and hung ceremoniously off her chest, while her eyebrows were reoriented into a permanently stern downward angle.

Finally, Chief Medal Officer Zooble was also present at Jax’s insistence - they served no official diplomatic function, but the captain had a hunch that the good doctor’s earnestness would help demonstrate to the Halkans just what the Federation was all about. They were also his friend. For this mission, they’d assembled with parts that were as symmetrical and uniform as they could manage, a look they topped off with a radio antenna headpiece which functioned as a built-in communicator, as well as a blue plastic Starfleet insignia on their chest. They had their medical bag slung over their shoulder, also “just in case”.

Our noble argonauts stood atop a ceremonial pad in the center of the tree-laden council chambers regarding the seven Halkan officials lining the room behind a long, curved table. Natural light poured in from the open sky above, reflecting warmly in the gelatinous, translucent flesh of the council. Their leader, Gummigoo, had been cordial but firm in his opposition to the deal all day, and it seemed that there was little else to be done.

This screenplay of a backstory flashed into the brains of the cast, passing almost as real memories, things which only on close examination fell, nauseatingly, into the uncanny. For several excruciating seconds, all they could do was stand gasping, trying to reconcile the experiences just grafted onto their minds. Jax’s eyes blurred as her-h-h-his heart thumped. This isn’t real. This isn’t real.

Gummigo addressed them. “I think I can say honestly that I believe you, Captain Kirk, but I just can’t change my mind. The council can’t let you mine diglucose crystals from our planet, not for any price.”

The short-armed Halkan to his left chimed in, “These fused elements, maple syrup and molasses, enable awesome energy - energy that can end many lives in the wrong hands.” He looked into his little palms and waggled his fingers dramatically.

Gummigoo nodded solemnly and continued. “We just can’t take the risk. If me mum found out I’d trusted the wrong people with this kind of power, she’d be heartbroken.”

“And you’d be face-broken!” a silvery-gray council member piped up from the end of the table.

“Easy, ma! I’m saying no!”

The dialog went almost entirely beneath Jax’s notice as his mind raced to make sense of the situation. So, we’re, what, LARPing? Could there be LARPing in hell? His pupils widened, out of focus.

Ribbit approached him and hesitantly put a hand on his shoulder. “Jax, are you alright?”

“I’m so confused,” he whispered, his stomach threatening to empty its contents onto the ground. “But, I’m okay, I think. I- I should be more alarmed about being trapped in a simulation, right? Why aren’t I more alarmed?”

“Don’t worry, I’m alarmed enough for both of us,” offered Zooble, turning on their medical tricorder. “And, according to this, you’re not exactly calm, either. Though, maybe this is just a normal heart rate for a rabbit. D🤬n it, I’m a bartender, not a doctor!”

“What’re y’all chattering about down there? Will you go in peace? Or are you going to use all those fancy weapons on your ship to take the crystals by force?” Gummigoo challenged.

Jax looked up with a spreading grin on his face, pupils still dialated. “Use force? What like, phasers and photon torpedoes? We-he-hell, that at least sounds-”

“Illogical!” Interjected Ribbit, “We would never use force to impose our will on a peaceful planet - that is not the Starfleet way.” She nudged Jax and whispered, “I think this’ll go more easily if we play along. Don’t worry, I know this one.” Her eyes widened as memory flashed through them. “U-uh, wait, uh oh, this is the one where-”

She was interrupted as a twinkling star from above suddenly crashed down into the city beyond, sending a massive plume of smoke up over the courtyard which dimmed the sun. The ground quaked beneath them; Zooble, unsteady on their new eclectic limbs, collapsed to the floor.

“Treachery, I knew it!” shouted Gummigoo, pointing accusatorily at Jax.

“You mean I knew it!” scolded his mother, shaking her head. Another missle crashed into the ground.

Zooble tapped their insignia to activate the communicator. “Away team to Enterprise, cease fire! Cease fire!”

Kinger’s voice crackled over the comm, unusually focused. “We’re not firing! Another ship just warped into the system and started bombing!”

“Scot- uh, who’s chief engineer?” Asked Ribbit.

“Kaufmo,” Jax said confidently, recalling the scenario memories. “Wait, how do I know his name?”

Just then, the sillhouettes of three figures glowing with transporter energy appeared just behind the away team, gradually solidifying into two heavily-armed mannequins and a tall, plump, cartoon pigeon. Their uniforms, blue for the mannequins and purple for the pigeon, were nearly identical to those of the Enterprise crew; the main exception was that their insignias were of a stylized Earth pierced by a mean-looking dagger. The menacing trio opened fire immediately, vaporizing the two mannequin Enterprise crewmen with garbled Wilhelm screams.

Crouching instinctively, Ribbit flipped open her communicator. “Beam us up, Kauffy! Now!”

“Energizing!” Kaufmo’s distorted voice responded, and Jax’s field of vision was instantly covered in a million stars like static, buzzing electrically as his entire form tingled violently. He blinked and it was over - he found himself staring at a concerned-looking Kaufmo behind the controls in the teleporter room.

“Jax!” he called, darting out from behind the console and jumping up onto the pad. “Jax are you okay? Do you know who I am?” He grasped the captain’s shoulders as he looked deep into his eyes, searhing for his friend.

“I… maybe,” said Jax, recoiling slightly.

“Let’s just take it slow,” said Ribbit in a soft tone that urged Kaufmo to turn down the intensity a little. “Right now, I think we should focus on the adventure in front of us.”

“Of all the green-blooded things to say,” growled Zooble, “don’t you care about your friend at all? Can’t you see that this is traumatic for her?”

Ribbit’s face hardened. “You don’t know us, Zooble. I love Jax more than you can imagine - I’m just trying to be logical in how we deal with all of this.”

“So it is ‘her’!” exclaimed Kaufmo, “congrats, man!” he said and clapped Jax on the arm. “Or uh, I mean ‘slay, queen!’” He smiled awkwardly, a slight pain in his eyes.

“Why are you all calling me a girl, I’m not a girl!” spat Jax. “I left that behind, I-I… get out of my head! All of you!” he stumbled back against the wall.

“Sorry, Jax, we’re sorry,” Ribbit spoke carefully as she approached. “You’ve had some memory loss. I-I don’t want to panic you. We can call you ‘he,’ It’s just, hard to shift gears.”

The intercom whistled. “Condition red - stand by all hands. Captain to the bridge,” echoed Kinger’s voice off the setpiece walls. The lights dimmed, and a siren blared dramatically.

“I, uh, I don’t know why I’m saying this,” said Jax, righting himself and looking her in the eye, “but I trust you. I understand what’s happening well enough to not trust myself right now. So I’m… trusting you.”

Ribbit smiled and took a step forward to grab his hand from his side, squeezing it warmly. “You’re safe with me, Jax.”
